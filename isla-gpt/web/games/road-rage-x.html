HOW TO PLAY

Drive fast, dodge traffic. Squeeze past cars for NEAR MISS points, chain them for a combo, hold brake while turning at speed to DRIFT, and hit NITRO for a huge boost. Near misses and drifts refill your nitro.

Modes: Highway Rush (go as far as you can), Street Race (beat 3 racers over 4 km), Police Chase (don't get boxed in and BUSTED, make cops crash into traffic) and Speed Challenge (reach each checkpoint before the clock hits zero). Crash too hard and your car is WRECKED.

Computer: W/↑ gas, AD/←→ steer, Space or S brake (and drift), Shift NITRO, C camera, Esc pause, M mute.

Phone or tablet: turn it sideways. ◀ ▶ to steer (or switch to TILT in Settings), GAS, BRAKE and NITRO on the right. Auto-gas is on by default on phones.

Garage: spend coins on new cars, paint, underglow and upgrades. Finish missions for bonus coins.
