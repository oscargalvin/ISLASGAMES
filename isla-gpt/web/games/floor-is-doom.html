HOW TO PLAY

SURVIVE THE FLOOR

Every round the floor changes. Watch the warning and the flashing tiles, then get to safety before the countdown hits zero. Lava burns, vanishing and crumbling tiles drop you into the pit, ice is slippery, slime makes you slow. Meteors smash tiles, lasers sweep the floor (jump them!), earthquakes shake you around and sometimes the lava RISES, so climb the tall pillars or ride the floating platforms (they crumble if you stand on them too long).

🎡 DOOM WHEEL

Every 20 seconds the Doom Wheel spins and picks a disaster: lava, ice, earthquake, meteors, vanishing floor or something special to the arena. The 🪑 spinning stool in the middle is a safe spot, but stay too long and it shouts GET OFF OR YOU WILL DIE! with a 5 second countdown. Jumping on it does not reset the timer.

WIN

Be the last runner standing against 10 computer players. Bump them with a sprint to knock them around. Grab coins to buy characters and upgrades, and grab power-ups: 🛡 shield, 👟 double jump, ⚡ speed and 🧲 magnet.

CONTROLS

2 Players (one keyboard): Player 1 uses WASD, Space jump, Left Shift sprint, Q E or the mouse to turn. Player 2 uses the arrow keys, Enter jump, Right Shift sprint, , . to turn. Last one standing wins!
Computer: WASD move, mouse to look (click first), Space jump, Shift sprint, Esc pause, M mute.
Phone or tablet: turn it sideways. Joystick on the left, swipe on the right to look, big JUMP and SPRINT buttons.
