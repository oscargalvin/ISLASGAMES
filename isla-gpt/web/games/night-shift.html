SHIFT

by Isla & Oscar's Games

Five nights at the Midnight Mart, ending on Halloween. You're working alone. Strange customers keep knocking, and some of them aren't human. Each night brings new jobs and creepier things. Survive until 6 AM to unlock the next night.

YOUR SHIFT

THE JOB

When someone knocks, inspect them through the window by the door desk, or from anywhere with the CCTV. Check their ID, ask questions, then press LET IN or DENY. Letting in a monster or turning away a real person is a mistake. Too many mistakes and it's over. Monsters you let in will hunt you, so run, hide in the staff locker or the storeroom boxes, and close doors behind you. Monsters can't open closed doors.

SPOT THE MONSTERS

  1. ID not expired (check tonight's date at the top of the rules)
  2. Photo matches: hair colour and glasses
  3. Spoken name matches the ID
  4. Spoken age and birthday match the ID
  5. 5 fingers on each hand
  6. They blink
  7. No glowing eyes on CCTV
  8. No twitching
  9. Normal smile, sensible answers

CONTROLS

Computer: WASD move, mouse look, Shift run, E use, Space or click to swing the bat, C CCTV, Q inspect the door, F torch, Esc back or pause, M mute. Click the buttons to decide.
Phone: turn sideways. Joystick to walk, swipe to look, and big USE, HIT, CCTV, RUN and LIGHT buttons.

FIGHT BACK

You've got a baseball bat. If you let a monster in while a shopper is inside, it goes for them, so run over and hit it to break up the fight before it eats them. Think someone is a monster? Hit them first, but if they're a real person they'll call the police, and you'll be arrested and fired.

RUN THE SHOP

Shoppers buy real food off the shelves and pay you. If something sells out, they leave unhappy. When the delivery truck pulls up out front, go to the front doors to buy crates of stock. Watch out for robbers in black masks: they sneak in and steal. Hit them with the bat to make them drop it and run. Sometimes a shopper fakes being hit to get you in trouble, but the CCTV never lies.

JOBS

Every night has different jobs: lock the back door, mop spills, restock, check the freezer, take out the rubbish, count the till, check the cameras, fix lights and answer the phone. Reset the breaker when the power cuts out. Beat a night to unlock the next one.
