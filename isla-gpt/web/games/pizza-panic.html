HOW TO PLAY

Deliver pizzas! Follow the arrow and the glowing beams to each customer's house. Get close and press E (or THROW PIZZA) to throw it to their door. Faster delivery = bigger tips. Throw at full speed for a DRIVE-BY bonus!

Keep your pizzas on! Your pizza boxes are stacked on your ride and they wobble. Hard turns, crashes and big jumps can send them flying. Out of pizzas? Drive back to the pizza shop (red beam) to restock and repair.

Zombies want your pizza. Smash through them at speed for BONK points. Go too slowly and they hop onto your ride and eat your pizzas! FIGHT them off: tap 👊 FIGHT (or press F) to whack them until they fall off. Watch out for skeletons too. They are faster, but only need 2 whacks. No delivery nearby? THROW PIZZA bonks zombies too.

Your shift ends if 3 orders arrive too late or your ride gets wrecked. Grab power-ups: 🌶 boost, 🧄 garlic shield, 🧀 cheese bomb, 🧲 coin magnet, ⏰ extra time.

Computer: WASD or arrows to drive, Space brake, Shift boost, E deliver / throw, F fight off zombies, H horn, Esc pause.

2 players (one keyboard, split screen): Player 1 drives with WASD, Left Shift boost, E throw, F fight. Player 2 drives with the arrow keys, Right Shift boost, Enter throw, / fight. You share the orders and the cash, so work together! A wrecked ride comes back at the pizza shop.

Phone: turn it sideways. ◀ ▶ steer, GAS, BRAKE, BOOST and THROW PIZZA on the right. A big 👊 FIGHT button pops up when a zombie jumps on. Auto-gas is on by default.
