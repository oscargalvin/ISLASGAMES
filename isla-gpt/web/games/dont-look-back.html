Midnight. You're locked inside the old school. Find the 5 hidden keys and unlock the main exit. Something tall is in here with you. It only moves when you're not looking.

How to survive

The rules

THE WATCHER freezes while you look at it. Turn away and it comes for you. It gets faster with every key you pick up. Use LOOK BACK to spin round instantly when you hear breathing behind you.

One key is hidden in each area: Library, Science Lab, Classroom 1A, Classroom 1B and the Basement. Golden glows are keys, green glows are batteries. Hide in a locker with the yellow handle if it gets too close, but don't let it see you get in.
