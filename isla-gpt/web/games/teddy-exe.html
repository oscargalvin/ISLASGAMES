HOW TO SURVIVE

Escape the toy factory. Find 3 fuses and put them in the fuse box to power the EXIT gate. Then find the two halves of the 4-number exit code written on notes, and type it into the gate. Coloured keys open coloured doors.

TEDDY hunts you. It hears running, rattling doors and machines. It sees your flashlight. Crouch to sneak, turn the light off to hide in the dark, hide in cupboards, or crawl into vents where it can't follow. But if it sees you climb into a cupboard… it knows.

Computer: W A S D move, mouse to look (click the game first), Shift run, C crouch, F flashlight, E use / hide, Esc pause.

Phone or tablet: turn it sideways. Left thumb moves, swipe on the right to look. RUN, CROUCH, LIGHT and USE buttons are on the right.

4 endings to find. Some secrets are only reachable through the vents…
