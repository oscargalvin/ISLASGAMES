Collect magical ingredients, brew wild potions, turn into a frog or a DRAGON, battle monsters and adopt the cutest pets!

HOW TO PLAY

1. Collect ingredients. Walk over flowers, mushrooms, dewdrops and more to pick them up.

2. Brew potions. Go to the bubbling cauldron and mix 2 or 3 ingredients. Try lots of mixes! New recipes go in your Potion Book. Some recipes are SECRET…

3. Drink potions from your bag. You can turn into a frog, chicken, slime, bat or dragon. Each one can go places a wizard can't! Some potions summon monsters to battle, and some summon pets to adopt.

4. Explore! Earn coins from monsters, treasure chests and quests, then use the magic portal to unlock new places.

Keyboard: WASD or arrows move, Space cast spell (or your creature's power), E use, 1–6 drink potion, X change back, B potion book, T pets, Q quests, P or Esc pause, M sound. You can also click to cast at the mouse.

Phone or tablet: drag on the left to move, ✨ casts, ✋ uses, tap a potion to drink it.
