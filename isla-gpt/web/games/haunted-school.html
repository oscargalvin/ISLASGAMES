HOW TO ESCAPE

Get out of the school! The front doors need power and the front door key. Find 3 fuses for the fuse box, then find the key. Each fuse is hidden behind a puzzle: a cupboard code, a song on the piano, and a secret bookshelf. Read every note and chalkboard for clues.

Miss Grimsby floats around the school. She hears running and wrong piano notes, and she spots your flashlight from far away. Sneak to be quiet, turn your light off to hide in the dark, and jump into a blue locker in the hall to hide. But if she SEES you climb in… she will open it!

Computer: W A S D move, mouse to look (click the game first), Shift run, C sneak, F flashlight, E use / hide, Esc pause.

Phone or tablet: turn it sideways. Left thumb moves, swipe on the right to look. RUN, SNEAK, LIGHT and USE buttons are on the right.
